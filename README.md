# Smart Medicine Reminder

An offline Android app that reminds you to take your medicines, with a full-screen
alarm that rings even when the phone is locked. No account, no internet needed.
All data stays on your phone.

## Features
- Alarm set by meal time plus or minus minutes (for example, 30 minutes after lunch)
- One medicine can have several meals; courses of N days or "Always"
- Photo and dosage of the medicine on the alarm screen
- Snooze, with a length you choose
- Daily dashboard that shows which doses are taken, upcoming or missed
- Optional follow-up reminder if a dose is not marked as taken
- Light and dark mode

## Download
Go to **Releases** (right side of this page) and download `app-release.apk`.

## Install
1. Open the downloaded file on your Android phone.
2. If asked, allow **Install unknown apps** for your browser or file manager.
3. If Google Play Protect shows a warning, choose **Install anyway**.
4. Open the app and allow notifications and alarms when it asks.

## Make sure alarms ring
Some phones stop background apps. In the app, open **Settings** and follow the
battery guide for your phone brand (Xiaomi, Samsung, Oppo, Vivo and others).
Use **Test alarm (1 min)** to check that it rings.

## Important
This app is a reminder only. It is not medical advice and does not replace your
doctor's or pharmacist's instructions.
